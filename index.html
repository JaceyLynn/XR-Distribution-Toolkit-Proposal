The problem

1 Artists don't consider and plan for distribution from the beginning of their project. Early decisions determine where a work can go later. Answered by Guided journey
2 Finding venues that fit a work means wading through a big table or a hard-to-read diagram. Plain filtering doesn't say where to start. Answered by Pathway
