The problem

1 Artists don't know where or how they could show their work, or what to plan for. “What's the doorbell?” Answered by Guided journey
2 Finding venues that fit a work means wading through a big table or a hard-to-read diagram. Plain filtering doesn't say where to start. Answered by Pathway
